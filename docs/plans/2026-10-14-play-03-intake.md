## Engineering design constraints already established

- Client inputs use only current viewer references. Raw-ID submit remains internal. Resolve references in the worker against the same committed checkpoint used for the proposed transition. Persist the original semantic wire intent for idempotency; identical reconnect retry must find its durable receipt before references are resolved again. A rejected/stale intent needs a new command ID when reconsidered.
- Qualify a new integer wire revision for the expanded viewer/actions, while retaining protocol1's diagnostic decoder and existing match pins. Do not widen advertisements or reinterpret old persisted requests. Pin/release/retention migration must be explicit.
- Obtain a short-lived, single-use ticket through authenticated HTTP; send it in the first socket authentication frame, never the URL. Bind ticket to principal/session/match/origin/release/protocol and consume it atomically across hosts. Verify participant membership and current session validity before private state or commands. No client actor/role override or privileged observer endpoint.
- Use full authorized committed snapshots. Version and per-connection sequence are distinct; gaps cause resync. Snapshot caching is bounded by bytes/count/TTL and keyed by exact version, checkpoint identity, release/engine pins, viewer and policy. Coalesce duplicate work; authorize and verify current committed identity before serving a cached view. Never reuse a projection for another actor.
- Cross-host publication is driven by durable metadata and reconciliation. PostgreSQL notification is only a hint; missed/doubled/reordered notification cannot lose a durable version. No Redis or additional paid service is required by the approved architecture.
- Bound pre-auth connections, auth deadlines, frame bytes, messages/rates, pending operations, outbound bytes, worker work, cache and reconnect attempts. Slow clients disconnect/resync rather than accumulating snapshots. Heartbeat/drain/session-expiry cleanup must reap only owned resources. Disconnect never concedes.
- Client transport owns pending semantic intents, receipt reconciliation, bounded jittered retry, refresh after suspension and stale-reference discard. Final game UI/art direction belongs to WEB/UX; this task supplies a browser-safe reusable transport plus actual client evidence.
- The 4,096-event projector limit remains fail-closed. Measure representative synthetic cost without claiming a real-card limit; all-card event/visibility/timing certification remains its owning later gate. Anonymous opponent references must continue producing hidden-identity-independent rejection.
